## Design Context

### Users
Targeted at all types of hospitality customers, including both employers (from QSRs to luxury hotels) seeking talent and employees seeking jobs. The primary context is highly mobile-first, with users typically browsing, applying, or managing candidates on the go.

### Brand Personality
Vibrant, approachable, and fast.

### Aesthetic Direction
Strict adherence to the visual language defined in `DESIGN.md` (deep teal hero bands, bright green CTAs, stark white surfaces, pill buttons, Euclid Circular A). The functional experience should be fast and utility-focused like Indeed, but with this specific vibrant UI. Must support both Light and Dark modes. Include i18n support for English and Nepali.

### Design Principles
1. **Mobile-Optimized:** Prioritize mobile layouts, large touch targets, and fast interactions for on-the-go users.
2. **Strict System Adherence:** Follow `DESIGN.md` explicitly—use pill buttons (`rounded.full`), brand green for primary CTAs, and deep teal for hero sections.
3. **High Utility & Speed:** Workflows (like applying or posting jobs) must be frictionless, fast, and highly approachable.
4. **Inclusive by Default:** Build with full Light and Dark mode support, and English/Nepali internationalization.
